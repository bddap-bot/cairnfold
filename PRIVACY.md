# Privacy

Nestcombe collects no personal data and sends nothing to its makers or to any server they run.
It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

## What stays on your device

The game keeps its own files in its app folder on your device: your garden, the photos you take,
the visitor name you pick, avatar files you add, and, only if you turn it on, a record of your
play sessions. None of them leaves the device except as described below.

## What goes to other players

- On your local network the game announces your garden so nearby players can visit it. The
  announcement carries a random key, your avatar choice and colour; it carries your visitor
  name only once you have picked one.
- When players visit each other, the game exchanges with them, directly over the local network:
  head and hand positions, the edits made to the garden, the garden itself, and the avatar file
  you wear.
- In a Steam copy launched by Steam, your Steam friends who also play can see, through Steam,
  that your garden is open; Steam's own privacy policy covers that.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the game deletes its folder and everything in it. Once a visit ends, the other players
keep nothing from it but the photos they took in the garden. Questions or requests go to the project's issue tracker:
https://github.com/bddap-bot/nestcombe/issues
